SpoolCut for Windows 11 x86-64

Extract the entire ZIP. Open bin\SpoolCut.exe to use the editor, or run
bin\spoolcut-cli.exe from a terminal for the command-line tool. Keep the bin,
lib and share folders together. FFmpeg and ffprobe ship in the bin folder.

SpoolCut is licensed under GPL version 3. See LICENSE and share\licenses
for notices. MSYS2-packages.tsv identifies the native packages in this build.
The accompanying SpoolCut-Windows-sources.tar.zst contains the exact SpoolCut
revision, Cargo dependencies, and corresponding MSYS2 source archives.

Build and verification instructions are in docs\WINDOWS.md in the source.
