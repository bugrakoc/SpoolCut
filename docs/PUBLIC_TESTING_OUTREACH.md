**SpoolCut 0.4.x, a lossless video cutter and joiner, needs test footage**

I maintain SpoolCut, a GPL-3.0-only desktop editor for keyframe-aligned
cuts and joins. It copies compatible H.264 or HEVC video and AAC-LC audio
from MP4/MOV to MKV without re-encoding. I'd like to learn how it handles
footage from more cameras and capture tools, especially HEVC Main 10 and
variable frame rate. A quick report on an export that worked or failed
would help. Please describe the footage and result. Sharing footage is
optional. SpoolCut has no telemetry.

**Show HN: SpoolCut, a lossless timeline editor for trimming and joining video**

I built SpoolCut because I often wanted to remove a few minutes from
recordings and join the parts I wanted to keep. SpoolCut copies compressed
packets and limits trims and splits to safe keyframes. It’s GPL-3.0-only
and works with compatible MP4/MOV recordings. If you try it, I’d like to
hear which recording settings work, where the keyframe limitation gets
in your way, and how the preview behaves on your machine.

Downloads and source are on [GitHub](https://github.com/bugrakoc/SpoolCut/releases).
